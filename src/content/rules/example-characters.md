These characters demonstrate the range of humanoids, beasts, and monsters that can appear in Rift Worlds 13.

## Level 1

Level 1 characters have a single skill and no perks. Most characters in the world are greater than level 1.

### Market Vendor

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 1
- **Skills:** Trading

### Dog

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 1
- **Skills:** Hunting

### Gremling Thief

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 1
- **Skills:** Thievery

## Level 5

Level 5 characters have five skill points and two perks. They are already formidable.

### Marauder

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 1
- **Skills:** Slashing, Looting, Brawling, Drinking, Lying
- **Perks:** Speed, Multitarget

### Bull

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 4
- **Skills:** Charging II, Strength II, Pulling
- **Perks:** Health, Physical Damage

### Wrathhound

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 1
- **Skills:** Hunting II, Fangs, Claws, Smell
- **Perks:** Evasive, Lethality

## Level 10

Level 10 characters have ten skill points, five perks, and a skill cap of 3. It would take many level 1 characters to stand even the slightest chance against one.

### Green Sage

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 1
- **Energy:** 6
- **Skills:** Nature Magic III, Old Language, Ritual Magic II, Intellect, Spirit Magic II, Politics
- **Perks:** Energy II, Recuperation, Energy Shield, Magic Healing

### Elephant

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 7
- **Skills:** Trunk, Tusks, Trample II, Strength II, Thick Hide, Bellowing, Wisdom II
- **Perks:** Health II, Physical Damage, Action, Multitarget

### Reaper Beetle

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 1
- **Energy:** 3
- **Skills:** Armour III, Acid Spray II, Climbing, Senses, Mandibles, Flight II
- **Perks:** Resistance II, Energy, Lethal Energy II
