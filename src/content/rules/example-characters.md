These characters demonstrate the range of humanoids, beasts, and monsters that can appear in Rift Worlds 13.

## Level 1

Level 1 characters have a single skill and no perks. Most characters in the world are greater than level 1.

### Market Vendor

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 2
- **Offence:** None
- **Defence:** None
- **Tactical:** Trading

### Dog

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 2
- **Offence:** Hunting
- **Defence:** None
- **Tactical:** None

### Gremling Thief

- **Level:** 1
- **Health:** 2
- **Offence:** None
- **Defence:** Thievery
- **Tactical:** None

## Level 5

Level 5 characters have five skill points and two perks. They are already formidable.

### Marauder

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 4
- **Offence:** Slashing, Brawling
- **Defence:** Drinking
- **Tactical:** Looting, Lying
- **Perks:** Speed, Multitarget

### Bull

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 7
- **Offence:** Charging II, Strength II
- **Defence:** None
- **Tactical:** Pulling
- **Perks:** Health, Physical Damage

### Wrathhound

- **Level:** 5
- **Health:** 4
- **Offence:** Fangs, Claws
- **Defence:** None
- **Tactical:** Hunting II, Smell
- **Perks:** Evasive, Lethality

## Level 10

Level 10 characters have ten skill points, five perks, and a skill cap of 3. It would take many level 1 characters to stand even the slightest chance against one.

### Green Sage

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 6
- **Energy:** 6
- **Offence:** Nature Magic III
- **Defence:** Spirit Magic II
- **Tactical:** Old Language, Ritual Magic II, Intellect, Politics
- **Perks:** Energy II, Recuperation, Energy Shield, Magic Healing

### Elephant

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 12
- **Offence:** Tusks, Trample II, Strength II
- **Defence:** Thick Hide
- **Tactical:** Trunk, Bellowing, Wisdom II
- **Perks:** Health II, Physical Damage, Action, Multitarget

### Reaper Beetle

- **Level:** 10
- **Health:** 6
- **Energy:** 3
- **Offence:** Acid Spray II, Mandibles
- **Defence:** Armour III, Flight II
- **Tactical:** Climbing, Senses
- **Perks:** Resistance II, Energy, Lethal Energy II
