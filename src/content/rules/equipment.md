Characters gain one perk at every even-numbered level. Perks can be selected multiple times, and their benefits stack.

## Energy perks

| Perk | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Energy** | Gain +3 maximum energy. Spend energy before a roll for +1, or for +1 magical damage. Recuperate 1 energy at the end of your turn. |
| **Recuperation** | Recuperate +2 additional energy at the end of each turn, up to your maximum. |
| **Desperate Energy** | Recuperate +1 energy whenever you fail a skill check or contest on your turn. |
| **Energy Drain** | Regain +1 energy whenever you damage a creature. |
| **Lethal Energy** | Energy spent on damage deals +2 magical damage per rank instead. |
| **Haste** | Spend 1 energy to gain +1 action per rank. |
| **Energy Shield** | When you or a visible ally takes damage, spend 1 energy to block +2 damage per rank. |

## Health and healing perks

| Perk | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Health** | Gain +3 health per rank. |
| **Healing** | Once per turn, restore +1 health per rank to yourself or an ally in your zone. |
| **Extra Healing** | Use Healing +1 additional time per turn per rank. |
| **Magic Healing** | Spend energy to restore +2 health per energy to yourself or an ally you can see. |
| **Resistance** | Gain +1 damage resistance per rank; take that much less damage each turn. |
| **Evasive** | Gain advantage on +1 defensive check or contest per round per rank. |

## Damage perks

| Perk | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Physical Damage** | Deal +1 damage per rank with weapons, unarmed attacks, and other physical sources. |
| **Lethality** | Deal +2 damage per rank when attacking with advantage. |
| **Retaliation** | +1 time per turn per rank, attack back when attacked. |
| **Magic Damage** | Deal +1 damage per rank with magical effects. |
| **Multitarget** | Target +1 character per rank in a zone with an attack. |
| **Range** | Target characters +1 zone away per rank with attacks. |
| **Momentum** | Deal +1 damage per rank and distance moved on your first melee attack after moving. Apply it again if you move more than once. |

## Mobility and utility perks

| Perk | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| **Speed** | Gain +1 movement speed per rank. |
| **Dash** | Gain +1 movement per turn per rank. |
| **Action** | Reactivate one used offence or tactical skill per turn per rank. |
| **Clutch** | Gain +1 automatic success on a check per day per rank. Clutch cannot be used for contests. |
