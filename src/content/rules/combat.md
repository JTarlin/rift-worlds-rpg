## Turns and skill activations

On their turn, a character may activate each of their **offence** and **tactical** skills once. They may activate as many different skills as they possess. Defence skills are reactive and are not expended when used.

Each rank of the **Action** perk allows the character to reactivate one offence or tactical skill that they already used that turn.

A character can also move once per turn. Movement allows them to enter an adjacent zone; movement speed and the Dash perk can extend this.

## Using skills in combat

Every skill is categorized as **offence**, **defence**, or **tactical**. Each individual skill can be used once per round.

A character with Swordsmanship, Dancing II, and Charm II could:

- Attack once using Swordsmanship.
- Make one tactical roll using Charm.
- Use Dancing when attacked to improve a defence roll.

This character fights by jabbing with a sword while evading and misdirecting enemies through fancy footwork and guile.

## Tactical contests

Tactical skills control the shape and timing of battle. Use tactical contests to:

- **Gain advantage:** Oppose an enemy's tactical or defensive skill. On a win, gain advantage on the relevant follow-up roll.
- **Determine initiative:** At the beginning of combat, characters make a tactical contest. Resolve turns from highest result to lowest; tied results favor the side initiating the conflict.
- **Defend against tactics:** When another character attempts to feint, intimidate, reposition, distract, or otherwise create a tactical edge, oppose them with a relevant tactical skill.

Tactical actions do not deal damage by themselves unless an ability explicitly says otherwise.

## Attacks and defence

An attack is a contest. The attacker rolls 2d6 and adds relevant offensive skills. The defender rolls 2d6 and adds relevant defensive skills. The higher total wins, and a tie goes to the attacker.

The attack's damage comes from the weapon, effect, or ability used, plus applicable perk bonuses. Subtract damage resistance, then reduce health by the remaining damage.

## Unconsciousness and death

At zero health, a character falls unconscious. Healing can revive them during combat, and friendly characters can revive them after combat.

If an unconscious character takes further damage from a hazard or enemy, they die.

## Energy in combat

Before rolling, spend 1 energy for **+1 to the roll**. You may instead spend energy to deal **+1 magical damage** on an attack. Perks can improve these uses or unlock new ways to spend energy.

Characters with an energy maximum recuperate 1 energy at the end of their turn unless another perk changes that amount.

## Zones and targets

Characters occupy distinct zones. Most movement reaches an adjacent zone. Offence and tactical skills can target characters in the same zone by default. Each rank of the Range perk increases their targeting distance by one zone. Multitarget allows an attack to affect additional characters in one zone.
